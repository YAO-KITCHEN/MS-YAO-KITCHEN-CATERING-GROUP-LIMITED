Windborne Trail

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

Your journey is yours.

Offline by design

Windborne Trail is an offline single-player game. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, remote crash reporting or cloud services. The application does not request internet access and does not transmit personal information.

Information collection and sharing

We do not collect personal data through this application. We do not sell or share information with third parties. No third-party advertising or analytics SDK is included.

Progress on your device

Level progress, scores, stars and preferences are stored in the application's private local storage. They remain on your device. The application disables operating-system backup for its data. Clearing application data or uninstalling the application deletes this local progress. No cloud recovery is available.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other players. There are no social, messaging or account features.

Your choices

You may turn music, sound and haptic feedback on or off. You may remove local information through Android's application storage settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact sajadali.202415@gmail.com. If you email us, your email provider and our email provider will process that message; the game itself sends no email or other communication.

Updates

Changes to this policy will be included in application updates with a revised effective date. This local document requires no network access.
